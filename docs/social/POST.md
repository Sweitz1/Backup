## Long (Reddit r/SillyTavernAI, RayNeo Discord)

**VeniceX3: SillyTavern-style AI characters on the RayNeo X3 Pro**

I built an unofficial Android app that puts AI chat on the X3 Pro's displays, using Venice AI.
You tap the temple, speak, and the answer shows in both eyes.

**What it does**
- **Characters:** a built-in companion (Mira), or import any SillyTavern / TavernAI card (`.png` or `.json`, V1–V3). Greetings, personality, scenario and `{{char}}`/`{{user}}` all work.
- **Card stores:** import straight from a Chub.ai or Character Tavern link, or from a direct card link from CharaVault, AI Character Cards or GitHub.
- **Pictures:** say "show me…", "draw…" or "send me a selfie" to get an AI image on the display.
- **Avatars:** a character image or a looping video beside the chat, in compact or portrait layout.
- **Controls:** temple gestures, plus optional on-device read-aloud.
- **Mature mode (18+):** off by default, unlocked only by confirming your age over adb, with separate switches for text, images and video. Mature cards involving minors are refused.
- **Privacy:** chats stay in memory only, and the request log keeps metadata only.
